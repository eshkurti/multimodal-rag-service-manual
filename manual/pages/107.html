Reset CBS Condition Based Service



CBS Reset

Sequence of the test module
- Check Tester data

1. CBS reset selection 1
1. Reset engine oil
2. Reset spark plugs or diesel particle filter or diesel additive
3. Reset front brakes
4. Reset rear brakes
5. Coolant
2. CBS reset selection 2
1. Reset brake fluid
2. Reset microfilter
3. Correction statutory vehicle inspection
4. Correction statutory exhaust-gas test
5. Vehicle check
3. End

The service tasks listed in CBS reset selection 1 and CBS reset selection 2 include all service tasks. The service tasks displayed in the test module can deviate from this scope depending on the type of vehicle and vehicle equipment.
