CBS Correction, Tester Data



CBS Correction Tester Data

CBS correction vehicle data serves to adjust the CBS scopes in the event of an incorrectly performed CBS reset. The service counter of the corrected CBS scope is decreased by 1

IMPORTANT: The original values of the CBS scope are lost during the correction

NOTE: The internal vehicle trip odometer and the vehicle data are coordinated with the internal Tester data. The Tester data can be corrected in the item "Administration" in the DIS Tester main menu.
