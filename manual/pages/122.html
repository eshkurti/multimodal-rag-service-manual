Engine Oil Service



Engine Oil Service

- Change engine oil and oil filter.
Recommended BMW High Performance 5W-30 Synthetic Oil, available under part number 07 51 0 017 866

- At every engine oil service
Replace A/C system microfilter (cabin filter)

- At every 3rd engine oil service:
Replace engine air intake filter (engine air filter)
