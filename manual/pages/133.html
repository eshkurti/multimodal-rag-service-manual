30000 MI or 48000 KM

Reset
    Service Reminder Indicators
      Reset CBS Display
Check
    Instrument Panel, Gauges and Warning Indicators
      Check 'check control' messages. Check indicators and warning lights.
Diagnose/Test
    Data Link Connector
      Perform diagnostic quick test.
Inspect
    Wiper and Washer Systems
      Headlamp washer function and windshield washer function. Windshield wipers and washer fluid level and concentration.
    Vehicle
      Final inspection (road test): brakes, steering, transmission, shock absorbers (visual)
    Tires
      Inspect tires for damage, wear, inflation pressure, condition. Record tread depth.
    Steering
      Check play, clearance, leaks, damage and wear
    Seat Belt Systems
      Safety belts: condition and function
    Power Steering Fluid
      Check level
    Parking Brake System
      Check parking brake condition, check parking brake lining thickness and function
    Mirrors
      Rear view mirror function
    Lighting and Horns
      Instrument cluster lighting, headlamps, tail lamps, marker lamps, turn signals, back-up, license plate, interior, map reading, sun visor, glovebox, luggage area, and flashlight
    Horn
    Hazard Warning Flasher
    Fluids
      Inspect underbody for visble signs of fluid leakage
    Dimmer Switch
    Coolant
      Coolant level and concentration. Record freeze point.
    Convertible Top
      Lower top, activate automatic roll-over protection system via diagnostic link
    Brake Rotor/Disc
      Rear Brakes
    Brake Hose/Line
      Brake system connections and lines: check for leaks, damage, and wear
    Body and Frame
      Check for damage and corrosion.
    Battery
      State of battery charge (magic eye), recharge if needed
    Air Bag Systems
      SRS airbag units (visual) for torn covers, obvious damage or attachment of stickers
Replace
    Oil Filter, Engine
    Engine Oil
    Cabin Air Filter / Purifier
    Brake Pad
      Rear Brakes
